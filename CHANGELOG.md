# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (test build)
- First version: new sandbox maps with "USA" in the name are built as the lower 48 states.
- Real coastlines, lakes and rivers (water and ocean), elevation for the terrain height, land cover for vegetation, 313 cities as towns, interstates as highways, real military bases, nuclear plants, airports, ports and labs.
- 128 x 80 chunk map, spawn in central Kansas.
