[h1]United States Map[/h1]
Play ZED ZONE on the United States. The lower 48 states are built from real world data and scaled down to fit one big map.

[list]
[*][b]Real coastlines[/b], the Great Lakes, big lakes and major rivers.
[*][b]Real hills and mountains[/b] from elevation data: the Rockies, Appalachians, Sierra Nevada and more.
[*][b]Real land cover:[/b] forests, farmland and desert where they really are.
[*][b]313 real cities[/b] as towns, bigger cities take more space (New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Houston...).
[*][b]Interstates[/b] as highways between them.
[*][b]Real landmarks:[/b] military bases, nuclear plants, cargo airports, ports and labs at their real locations.
[*]You start in the middle of Kansas.
[/list]

[h2]How to play[/h2]
Make a new sandbox map and put [b]USA[/b] in its name (for example "USA run"). Every other map stays as normal.

[h2]Size[/h2]
The map is 128 x 80 chunks, twice as wide and tall as a normal map, so the first load takes longer and uses more memory.

[h2]Co-op[/h2]
Everyone should install it.
